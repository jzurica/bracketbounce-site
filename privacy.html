Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains how Bracket Bounce ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you use the Bracket Bounce iOS app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Apple or email, we receive your account identifier, email address (or Apple private relay address) and the display name you choose.
  • Gameplay data: your brackets, picks, scores, coin balance, boosts, friends and blocked players.
  • Purchase history: records of in-app purchases and subscriptions, used to deliver coins and VIP benefits. Payments are processed by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Device and advertising data: device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), ad interactions and product interaction data.
  • Coarse location: approximate location derived from your IP address, used by our ad partner to serve relevant ads. We do not collect precise location.
  • Crash data: diagnostic information to fix bugs.

How we use information

To run the game and your account, deliver purchases, show leaderboards, show rewarded video ads, measure and improve the app, prevent fraud and abuse, and respond to support requests.

Advertising and tracking

We use Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, ad interaction data and coarse location for advertising and analytics. iOS will ask whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads may be less personalized. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses data.

Service providers

We use Apple (sign-in, payments, Game Center), Google Firebase (accounts and data storage) and Google AdMob (ads). They process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information for money.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account anytime in the app (More > Account > Delete account). This removes your account and gameplay data from our servers, except records we must keep for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or US states such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL_TBD to make a request.

Children

Bracket Bounce is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
