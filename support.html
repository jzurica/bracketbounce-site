FAQ

How do I play?

Each week you get a bracket of 8 real games. Pick the winner of each First Round game. In later rounds, the team that advances is the one with the better result in its next real game. Lock in before the first game tips off.

How does scoring work?

Correct picks earn 10 points in the First Round, 20 in Round 2, 40 in the Semifinals and 80 in the Final. Most points wins the week. Your Final score prediction only breaks ties.

What are coins?

Each entry costs 100,000 coins. You get free coins when you sign up and can earn 25,000 coins per rewarded video (up to 5 a day). Coins can also be bought in the Shop. Coins have no cash value and can't be cashed out.

What are boosts?

  • Mulligan – change one pick after lock.
  • Double Down – double the points on one pick.
  • Pick Insurance – protect one pick.
  • Crowd Picks – see what other players picked.

Buy boosts in the Shop or while filling out a bracket. One of each per bracket per week.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel VIP?

On your iPhone open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Bracket Bounce VIP > Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to More > Account > Delete account. This permanently removes your account, brackets and coins.

How do I block a player?

Open their profile and tap Block.
